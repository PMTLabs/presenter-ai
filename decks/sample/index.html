PRESENTER-AI

Sample deck

A three-slide deck used to check that the presenter works end to end.

How it works

  • The controller shows a slide and sends its narration to GPT-Live.
  • The model speaks; you can interrupt with a question at any time.
  • After the narration ends, the next slide is shown automatically.

Keys

  • Space: pause / resume
  • Arrow right / left: next / previous slide
  • M: mute the microphone
  • Esc: end the session
